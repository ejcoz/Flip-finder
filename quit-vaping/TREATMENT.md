# You quit vaping — treatment (Pixel Scan)

36 s · 1080×1920 · 120 BPM (18 bars) · music and effects, no voice · English.

## Three candidate structures
1. **The clock** (stop-a-habit timeline): hook → 2 hours → days 1–3 (setback) → why it's hard (lens) → weeks 1–4 (cravings chart) → weeks 6–12 (brain resets, lens again) → month 3+ (calmer mind) → finale. Every beat is a time on the clock and one change.
2. **The tug of war**: two scanners, "still vaping" vs "quit", the same milestones side by side. Strong contrast, but halves the body size we just fought for and doubles the claims to source.
3. **The receptor story**: the whole film inside the zoom lens on the brain, receptors multiplying with use and dying back after quitting. Clear mechanism, but loses the body, which is the style's point.

**Choice: 1, the clock.** It is the format the audience knows, it keeps the big body, and it lets the lens appear twice as a rhyme (too many receptors → back to normal) rather than carrying the film.
