# Why Are Homes So Pricey in New Brunswick? — Treatment

Style: Mid-century Cartoon (`midcentury-toon`). Format: Instagram Reel, 1080×1920 vertical, 24 fps, ~60 s, English narration (AI voice, Kokoro af_heart), burned-in captions + `.srt`.

## 1. Three candidate structures

A. **The price tag that grows (chosen).** One coral house is the "product" for the whole film. Its price tag reads $172,200 (2019) and swells to $352,200. Three numbered medallions stamp on, and the camera goes through each one like a 1950s classroom film card: More People → Too Few Homes → Higher Costs. A see-saw of demand vs supply pays it off, and we end on the same house with a question.
B. **Do / don't split.** Two colour blocks: "2019 New Brunswick" vs "Today", each reason shown twice. Clear, but it makes a "why" into a comparison and halves the frame on a phone.
C. **A family's house hunt.** A couple tour houses as each reason knocks prices up. More emotional, but it needs a lot of character animation and dialogue for 60 s, and the numbers would compete with the story.

**Choice: A.** A reel needs one object people recognise at thumbnail size (the house + tag), a hook number in the first 3 s, and a list structure, which is the native grammar of the classroom film (numbered medallions, one sentence per picture). The opening price tag comes back at the end, so the film echoes.

## 2. Logline and arc

A little New Brunswick house watches its price tag double in seven years, while a calm 1950s-style narrator counts out the three reasons why.

- Setup: the house, $172,200 in 2019. The tag swells to $352,200. "It doubled."
- Turn: three reasons, each stamped as a medallion and entered through an iris. More people arrive; not enough houses get built; every house costs more to build.
- Ending: a see-saw tips (demand heavy, supply light). Back to the house, now with a question: "How do we build faster?" Held still, then an iris-out on the house.

## 3. Benchmark

- **1950s classroom/educational films** and **UPA-era flat cartoons**. Learn: one sentence per picture, numbered cards, colour-plane rooms, limited animation where only hands move, a calm narrator. Don't take: any characters, drawings, lettering or music.
- **Modern short-form explainers on vertical video**. Learn: a hook number in the first 2 s, big centred subjects, captions in the lower third clear of the subject, a new picture every 3–5 s. Don't take: layouts, logos or shots.

## 5. Narration (one clause per line, calm classroom authority)

1. In 2019, a typical New Brunswick home cost about $172,000.
2. Today? About $352,000.
3. It doubled. So, why?
4. Reason one: more people.
5. During the pandemic, buyers from Ontario and beyond discovered New Brunswick was a bargain.
6. Then came record immigration. The province grew by about 25,000 people in a single year.
7. Reason two: not enough homes.
8. The province says it needs 6,000 new homes a year.
9. In 2022, builders started about 4,680.
10. Reason three: building got expensive.
11. Lumber, labour and borrowing all cost more, so every new home costs more to build.
12. More people. Too few homes. Higher costs.
13. When demand outruns supply, prices only go one way.
14. So, New Brunswick: how do we build faster?

## 6. Beat sheet and cue map

Tempo 112 bpm, 4/4 (bar = 2.143 s). Final times lock to voice durations; sections start on a bar line.

- Intro (bars 1–4): brushes + walking pizzicato bass, vibraphone chord stabs. Tag swell on bar 3 downbeat: vibraphone glissando up, slide whistle.
- "So, why?": **silence**, half a bar: everything cut. The first sound after it is the medallion stamp ×3.
- Reason 1 (each reason gets its own vibraphone chord): flute melody enters; finger snaps; a pop per person arriving.
- Reason 2: halved feel; celesta on each house icon placed; a hollow "tock" for each dashed missing house.
- Reason 3: sticks on the ride (energy); wood knock (lumber), clank (hard hat), ding (%).
- Summary: the three reason chords return, one per item. **Near-silence** (room tone only) before "When demand outruns supply…"; see-saw thump is the first sound.
- Ending: muted-trumpet tag, vibraphone tremolo under the held frame, iris "shhk", final chord.

## 8. Type and captions

- Kicker in a 1950s script (Oleo Script Bold): "Why?", the closing question.
- Slab serif (Alfa Slab One) for prices, numerals and reason titles, ink over an offset colour plate.
- Geometric sans (Jost 500/600) for captions and labels.
- Caption card: a paper-coloured rounded slab with an ink border, small stars at the ends, ~54 px Jost (scaled for a phone), in the lower third at y ≈ 1440–1560, above Instagram's bottom UI. Subjects sit between y ≈ 260 and 1350, clear of the caption band and the top UI.
- Palette: cream paper ground, warm near-black ink. The house is always **coral**. Accent (presenter's scarf, stars) **mustard**. Grounds rotate: mustard (title), turquoise (reason 1), olive (reason 2), powder blue (reason 3), oatmeal (summary), cream (ending).
- No presenter: limited animation lives in the hands (a glove points at the 1,320-home gap in reason 2), the stamps and the pop-ins.
