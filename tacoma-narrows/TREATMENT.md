# Tacoma Narrows — treatment

Style: Blueprint. 1080×1920 vertical, 24 fps, 35.0 s. English voice (Kokoro `am_michael`, calm, speed 0.9), burned-in captions drawn as drawing notes, `.srt` exported. No user material; everything is drawn in code.

## Three candidate structures

1. **Single sheet, two figures (chosen).** One cyanotype sheet: FIG. 1 is the bridge as built, drawn in elevation; it is set in motion, twists and breaks *inside the drawing*. A red stamp gives the verdict. FIG. 1 is struck out and rewritten as FIG. 2, the wind-tunnel test, drawn lower on the same sheet. Ending: the camera pulls back to show the whole sheet, the broken bridge above the test that answers it.
2. **Revisions in the title block.** REV. A (opened) → REV. B (twisting) → REV. C (collapsed) → REV. D (tested). Clear but bookish; the collapse becomes a table row, not an event.
3. **Countdown of four months.** Calendar ticks from July to November in the notes column while the twist grows. Good tension, but the narration already compresses time; a calendar competes with the voice.

Chosen: 1. The drawing itself is the bridge, so the failure can be the medium breaking (lines snap, the deck drops off the paper), and the cure is a second drawing, not a caption.

## Logline and arc

A bridge drawn with perfect convention starts to move in the wind, twists itself apart, and its broken drawing becomes the reason the next drawing is a wind tunnel.
Setup: the elevation drafts itself, dimensions and opening date. Turn: wind lines, the deck turns from a flat line into a twisting ribbon (oblique projection opens 0 → 0.55), the section A-A tilts. Ending: snap, fall, silence, red stamp; FIG. 2 draws and the airflow passes cleanly over a steady model.

## Benchmark

- 19th-century patent sheets and the moving technical drawings of title sequences (grammar only). **Learn**: drafting order, live dimensions, part balloons, one stamp as verdict. **Don't take**: any marks, logos, shots or real patent drawings.
- Wind-tunnel smoke photographs of the 1940s–50s (as a reference for how streamlines look). **Learn**: evenly spaced streamlines, a model on springs. **Don't take**: any specific photo.
- No archival footage of the collapse is used or traced; the bridge is a simplified drawing (proportions approximate: main span 2,800 ft, side spans 1,100 ft, deck 39 ft wide with 8 ft solid plate girders).

## Subtitles and titles

Drawing notes: translucent box with double white rules, a "NOTE / n" cell, Architects Daughter lettered on left to right in ~0.35 s, a small leader tick, placed per shot in empty sky (never over the bridge or the tunnel). Each note is also lettered into the GENERAL NOTES column. Title-block fields in B612. The one non-blue colour: the red COLLAPSED stamp.
