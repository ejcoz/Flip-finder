# Enter the Bunker: lore bible

This is the canon for the bunker. Future videos should stay consistent with it. Things marked **(on screen)** already appear in the current film; the rest is background to draw on later.

## The bunker

- **What it is:** a cylindrical concrete silo buried under a ruined city, built to wait out a nuclear war. It has seven levels, **B1 at the top to B7 at the bottom (on screen)**, with a stair and lift core down the centre **(on screen)**.
- **Capacity:** 100,000 beds, about 14,300 per level. Every bed has a number. **Following** gets you a bed (the counter is the follower count); **commenting** picks where your bed is. Bed numbers restart on each level, so a bed's full name is `level-number`: your bed is `B5-0143`, the forbidden one is **bed 000** (`B8-0000`): beds are numbered from one, so it shouldn't exist either.
- **Who runs it:** the narrator ("we"). They found the bunker, dug it out and are filling it, but they didn't build it. Who did is unknown; that's an open question to use later.
- **Getting in:** a hatch and shaft from the surface **(on screen)**, plus a sloped tunnel that ends at the round **blast door with the radiation symbol (on screen)**. Two vent stacks with mushroom caps rise to the surface **(on screen)**.
- **The surface:** a ruined skyline under a rust-coloured smog sky, with ash always falling **(on screen)**. When the door seals, a mushroom cloud rises on the horizon **(on screen)**.

## The rule and the milestones (on screen)

The **blast door won't seal until every bed is full**. The counter on the wall shows `SURVIVORS INSIDE 0 / 100,000`.

| Survivors | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1,000 | The lights come on (bunks light up level by level) |
| 10,000 | The air starts flowing (the air scrubbers on B7 start; air moves up the vents) |
| 100,000 | The door seals, and everyone waits out the end of the world |

## The levels

### B1 — Entry and decontamination
- The airlock behind the blast door, the decontamination showers and quarantine bunks for new arrivals.
- The first level the diggers reached. Everything here was empty and unlocked, which is what made B8 so frightening later.

### B2 — Command, comms and medical
- The radio room (listening for other survivors), the periscope to the surface, and the master copy of the counter.
- The **med bay**: surgery, a pharmacy and the bunker's one doctor.
- The archives: the original builders' blueprints, which show **eight** levels.

### B3 — The garden (on screen)
- A ring of hydroponic beds under grow-lights: the only green thing left in the world.
- The seed vault and the water-recycling tanks under the planters.
- It's the warmest, brightest level, which makes it the most wanted place to sleep.

### B4 — Commons
- The mess hall and kitchen, and the **pantry**: tinned food stacked floor to ceiling, rationed by the counter.
- A school and library for the children, and a rec room with a projector and one box of old films.
- The bunker's social centre and where most of its gossip starts.

### B5 — Main dormitory
- The densest residential level: rows of bunks curving around the wall.
- **Your bed, B5-0143 (on screen):** gold, with your name on the door.

### B6 — Armory and security (on screen)
- Weapon racks along the back wall **(on screen)**, the security office and a small brig.
- The guards sleep here. Bunks near the armory feel safest, or most dangerous, depending on who you ask.

### B7 — Engineering and life support
- The **generators** and the reactor that power everything, the air scrubbers that start at 10,000 survivors, and water purification.
- It's loud and hot, and the workers who live here like the noise.
- A maintenance hatch in the floor, welded shut by the narrator's group after their one trip to B8. It's not on any current map.
- **The bed above the hatch (on screen):** the B7 bed directly over bed 000, as close as anyone is allowed to sleep. It's the dare option when picking your spot.

### B8 — The floor that doesn't exist (on screen)
- Officially there are seven levels **(on screen: "This bunker only has seven levels")**. B8 lies *below the foundation*, under the hazard-striped footing **(on screen)**. It only appears when the power fails and the red emergency lights come on.
- It's older than the rest and almost empty: a dark drum of cracked, stained concrete with scratch marks on the walls. **There is only one bed down there (on screen).**
- **Bed 000 (on screen).** The only bed on B8, glowing red, at the dead end of a long bare corridor with no other doors **(on screen)**. Its door is latched **from the inside** **(on screen)**, and after the latch slams, three knocks come back **(on screen)**. Nobody has opened it, and bed numbers start at 1, so its number shouldn't exist.
- **How anyone knows (on screen):** when the narrator's group first dug the bunker out, the hatch in B7's floor was **open**. They went down once, found B8 and bed 000 latched from the inside, and knocked. Something knocked back. They came up and **welded the hatch shut** ("So we welded the hatch shut."). Nobody has been down since.
- That's the narrator's guilty secret: they're filling the bunker with 100,000 people, knowing it isn't empty.
- The original blueprints in the B2 archives show eight levels.

## Story threads to use later
- **Who is in bed 000**, and why the knocks come in threes.
- **How B8 got there**: built first and hidden, or dug by whoever is inside it?
- **Who built the bunker**, and why for exactly 100,000 people.
- **What else is on B8.**
- **The counter:** does anything happen at numbers other than the milestones? (For example, comments going quiet at 000.)
- **Picking your spot:** each new video can offer two attractive rooms and one forbidden one, as this film does with the garden, the armory and 000.

## Visual key (for consistency)
- Concrete: `#A9A48C` / `#6C6855`. Bunk shells: `#2E3625`. Bed light strip: toxic green `#9CF04A`.
- Hazard yellow `#F2C230`: the dashed lines, counter numbers, stripes, the trefoil and level labels.
- Gold `#FFC23A` means **you**. Red `#FF3B3B` means **000 / the forbidden**. Green glow means the garden or the scanner.
- Sky: rust smog `#140E0A` → `#5A3417`, with falling ash.
