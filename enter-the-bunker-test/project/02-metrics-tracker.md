## What to watch
- **Skip rate:** below 25% is good; below 20% is great. This is Instagram's top ranking factor.
- **Share rate:** the weakest number. Aim for 0.5% or more.
- **Follow % of viewers:** the series' superpower. If it drops below 3%, the premise needs a fresh hook.
- **Retention drops:** screenshot the curve and note which line was playing at each step. Silences and long explanations show up as steps.
