# Brand kit

## Names
- **Account:** Join the Bunker · **Handle:** @jointhebunker
- **Series:** *Enter the Bunker* · Episodes are "Transmissions" (Transmission 02 …)
- Viewers are **survivors**. The narrator is "we". The elected leader is **the Overseer**.

## Voice
Calm, dry, a little ominous. A survivor running a shelter who knows more than they say. Short sentences. Never hype, never meta, no exclamation marks.
- Yes: "B6 it is. Keep your boots by the door."
- No: "OMG welcome to the armory!!! 🔥🔥"

| Title cards | Anton, the number in amber over a white line |

**The look:** a cylindrical concrete silo in cutaway, falling ash, and only lights glow. Never sci-fi or rocket-like.
**The profile picture:** the round blast door with the radiation trefoil and a hazard-stripe ring (`profile-pic/profile_a.png`).
**The sound:** a low drone, and three knocks to end every episode.
