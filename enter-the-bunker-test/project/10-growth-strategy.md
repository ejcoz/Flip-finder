# Growth strategy

## Where we stand
Episode 1 proved the hard part: **people follow** (9.9% of viewers) and **people comment** (5.7%). Instagram's algorithm did the distribution (93% from the Reels tab, 95% non-followers). The gaps: **shares (0.2%)**, **saves (0.4%)**, and the **first 5 seconds** (30% gone by 5 s).

## Priorities
### 1. Shares (Instagram's second-ranked factor)
People share when they have someone in mind, or when they appear in it themselves.
- **A named share prompt** each episode: "Send this to the one person you're bringing down." "Send this to whoever's in the bunk next to you."
- **Handles on screen:** roll calls and the Founders Wall. People send the video with their own name in it.
- **Bunkmates:** "Tag your bunkmate" in replies, and a later "bunkmate" mechanic where two friends share a room.
- **Votes that need allies:** the Overseer election (people recruit friends to like their nomination).

### 2. Saves
People save what they'll come back to.
- **The bed lookup page** and the line "Save this to find your bed later."
- **Lore recaps and maps:** a carousel post of the levels ("Know your bunker").
- **Clue tracker posts:** "Every clue so far." Theorists save these.

### 3. The first 5 seconds (skip rate, the top-ranked factor)
- Open on motion and the mystery: a counter glitch, a red flash, a knock.
- Put the most surprising line first: "We got one more than we let in."
- Keep the title card over moving video, never on a still.
- Test two different first lines on similar episodes and compare skip rates.

## Cadence
- An episode every 2–3 days, a short in between, stories most days.
- Post at a similar time of day to episode 1, then adjust using the best times shown in Insights.
- Don't post while the last post is still climbing fast.

## Other platforms
| Platform | Plan |
|---|---|
| **TikTok** | Same episodes, uploaded natively (no Instagram watermark). The audience skews young, which fits. Separate comments mean a separate registry platform. |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Same episodes, uploaded natively. Good long-tail discovery. Add a "Watch from episode 1" playlist. |
| **Instagram Stories** | For followers: polls, question boxes, counters. Highlights: JOIN, CLUES, BEDS. |

Start cross-posting once episode 2 is out, so new viewers elsewhere can catch up with two episodes.

## What not to do
- **Don't boost yet.** Organic reach is working; paid views convert worse and blur your stats.
- **Don't change the format suddenly.** Change one variable per episode.
- **Don't explain the mystery.** Theories make people comment and come back.
- **Don't mass-follow, mass-like or use engagement apps.** That risks another block.
- **Don't promise what the show can't deliver** (prizes, DMs, dates).
