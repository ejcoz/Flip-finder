# Community playbook

Comments are the show's engine: 954 on episode 1 in six hours. This is how to handle them without burning out or getting the account restricted.

## The voice in replies
Calm, dry and slightly ominous: a survivor running a shelter, not a brand. Short sentences. Never meta (no "algorithm", "content", "my video"). At most one emoji (🌱 garden, 🔫 armory, 🔒 or ☢️ otherwise). Creepiness is saved for B7, B8 and bed 0: hint, never explain. Full bank in `09-reply-bank.md`.

## Hard rules
- **No bed number unless the commenter named one.** Otherwise confirm the level ("B6 it is").
- Named number: "Bed 3,332 is yours. B1, near the decontamination showers." Over 100,000: "We only have 100,000 beds." Taken: "That bed's taken."
- **Bed 0 is never available.** Bed 100,000 goes to the first person who asks for it.
- **Never spoil** what's in bed 0, who built the bunker, or why the knocks come in threes.
- **Only promise what's real:** a bed for following, the Founders Wall (first 1,000), bed numbers at 1,000, and the milestones (lights 1,000, air 10,000, door 100,000).

## How much to reply
| Phase | What to do |
|---|---|
| First hour after a post | Reply quickly to the first 20–30 comments |
| After that | **20–30 replies a day**, in 2–3 short sessions |
| Over 500 comments | Stop trying to answer everyone. A pinned comment and the roll call recognise them all at once. |

**Reply to these first:**
1. Story questions
2. Named bed numbers
3. Funny or in-character comments
4. Comments with many likes
5. Regulars

**Skip** plain one-word picks. Like only a handful of comments: likes are rate-limited too.

**Why the limits matter:** a burst of 60+ similar replies and likes on a new account triggered a temporary action block on 21 November. Keep it slower and rotate the wording.

## Recognising everyone without replying to everyone
- **Pinned comment:** "Every survivor is logged. Bed numbers go out at 1,000. Look for your name in the next transmission."
- **Roll call in every episode**, plus the line "We read every name."
- **Bed lookup page** (planned, link in bio): anyone gets their bed number instantly.

## The Survivor Registry
https://claude.ai/artifact/N6gR6PpmPkssaAT8XYJEmL tracks every commenter: their spot, any named bed, when they were first seen, and whether you replied.
- **Fastest way to add comments:** on a computer, open the post on instagram.com, select all the comments, copy, and paste into the registry's Add box (From: Instagram). Or paste the text to Claude.
- **From your phone:** send Claude screenshots in big batches.
- Claude can then write your replies, choose roll calls, assign beds and build the Founders Wall from the registry.

## The Overseer
An elected survivor who "speaks for the survivors". **Status, not power:**
- Elected by the most-liked comment on the election post by the deadline (ties go to the earliest). Only followers can win, and the bunker can refuse an offensive winner.
- Gets: named on screen, top of the Founders Wall, and one **cosmetic** call per episode (name the dog, the pet rule, tonight's ration).
- Never decides plot. If a choice must look big, both options lead to the planned clue. The narrator can overrule ("The Overseer ordered the hatch opened. We said no.").
- Re-elected at each big milestone, so the election becomes a recurring event.

## Running jokes now in canon

- One personal item each; B4 is the soccer stadium.
- The armory filled first and is now **full**.
- The locked red crate on B6.

## Safety (a third of the audience is 13–17)
- Keep the horror PG-13: dread, sounds and red light. No gore.
- **Never ask for personal information** (real names, ages, locations, photos).
- **DMs:** avoid sending automated DMs to followers you don't know. Instagram limits messaging teens, and automation on a flagged account risks another block.
- **Contests:** the Overseer is a title, not a prize. If a real prize is ever offered, read Instagram's promotion rules first and add official rules.
- **Profanity:** skip handles or comments with swearing in roll calls and on screen.
- Hide or delete hateful comments. Don't reply to trolls; hiding is quieter.

## When something goes wrong
- **Wrong level in a reply:** reply again in character: "Correction, survivor. Clerical error. You're on B6."
- **Someone upset about not getting a reply:** "Every survivor is logged. Watch the next roll call."
- **Action block:** stop, wait 24–48 h, check Settings → Account Status, turn on two-factor authentication, then resume slowly.
