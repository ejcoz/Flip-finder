## The truth: the bunker has been filled before

1. **The first fill.** Long ago, the unknown builders filled the bunker with exactly 100,000 people. When the last bed filled, the blast door sealed, as designed.
2. **The +1.** At the moment it sealed, the counter read **100,001**: one more heartbeat than beds. Nobody could find the extra one.
3. **The emptying.** People began to disappear, one by one, until the bunker was empty. When the narrator's group dug it out, every level was empty, the beds made and the doors unlocked.
4. **The Keeper.** One person survived: the bunker's first keeper (the first **Overseer**), who ran it as the narrator does now. B8 is the oldest part of the bunker and the only room that locks from the inside. The Keeper locked themself in **bed 0** and scratched a tally of days into the walls.
5. **What's in bed 0 now.** The tallies stop. The Keeper died long ago. When the narrator knocked and something knocked back, it was **the extra one**, which copies what it hears.
6. **Now.** In episode 2 the counter reads **one more than they let in. Again.** The narrator assumes the extra heartbeat is bed 0. The audience slowly learns it may not be.
