# Roadmap and calendar

## The rhythm
- **One episode every 2–3 days** (40–50 s), numbered "Transmission 02", "Transmission 03" and so on.
- **One short post between episodes** (10–15 s): a vote, a single clue, a counter tick.
- **Stories most days:** polls, question boxes and milestone counters, mostly for existing followers.
- **Timing:** wait until the last episode's views have flattened (usually 24–48 h) before posting the next. Never post while the last one is still climbing fast.

## Every episode has
1. A hook in the first second, with the mystery in motion.
2. **One new clue** (see `04-secret-canon.md`).
3. **One milestone or community beat** (the lights, bed numbers, the Overseer, the full armory).
4. **A roll call:** 4–6 real survivors on screen.
5. **One comment ask**, with the type rotating each time.
6. An end that returns to the threat, then loops.

## In-between post ideas (cheap to make)
- **Counter tick:** 5 s of the counter rolling, a red blip, three knocks. Caption: "Did anyone else hear that?"
- **Level spotlight:** 10 s swoop through one floor, with a roll call of its survivors.
- **Bed lookup reminder:** "Find your bed. Link in bio." (once the bed lookup page exists)
- **Night shift:** the bunker with the lights off, one bunk lit on B7.
- **Poll story:** "Seal it or open it?" (feeds the finale)

## Tools to build next
1. **Bed lookup page (link in bio):** anyone types their handle and level and gets a permanent bed number. It's worked out from the handle, so it's the same every time, needs no replies, and fulfils the 1,000 promise for everyone at once. Stores nothing.
2. **Level leaderboard** in the Survivor Registry (counts per level, to read out on screen).
3. **Roll-call picker** in the registry: suggests 4–6 survivors who haven't been featured yet.
