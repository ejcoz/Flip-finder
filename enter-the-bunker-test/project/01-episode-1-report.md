# Episode 1 report: "100,000 Empty Beds"

**Posted:** 21 November 2026 · **Snapshot:** 6 hours after posting · **Length:** 54.6 s · **Platform:** Instagram Reels

## Verdict
A breakout first post. Episode 1 turned **one in ten viewers into a follower** and drew close to a thousand comments, while views were still accelerating when the snapshot was taken. The premise (a bed for following, a spot for commenting) works. Two things are weak: **sharing and saving**, and **the first five seconds**.

## When people liked it

- **13–14 s:** "Everyone who follows gets a bed with their name on the door". The core promise is the second big like moment.
- Smaller bumps at about 24 s and 27 s (the end of the world, and the turn to "something I haven't told you").

## What worked
1. **The premise is participatory:** a bed for a follow, a spot for a comment. The follow rate proves it.
2. **The visual:** one big bright structure filling the frame, with a clear title card.
3. **A twist in the second half** (B8, bed 0) holds everyone who reaches it.
4. **Concrete choices to comment** (garden, armory, B7) make commenting effortless.
5. **The profile converts:** 3 in 4 visitors followed.

## What to fix next time
1. **The first 5 seconds:** open on motion and the mystery, not only a title. Episode 2 opens on the counter glitching (see `06-episode-2-script.md`).
2. **No silences over about 0.5 s.** The 1.7 s pause cost about a tenth of the viewers.
3. **Give people a reason to share:** a named person to send it to ("the one person you're bringing down"), and their friends' handles on screen in the roll call.
4. **Give people a reason to save:** bed lookups, maps and lore recaps are things people come back to.
5. **Tease the twist earlier** in the voice, not just visually, so the 15 s exit is smaller.

## Promises now due
Episode 1 promised the **lights at 1,000** and, in replies, **bed numbers at 1,000**. Both are now owed. Episode 2 must pay them off, or trust drops.
