# The episode formula

Episode 1 got a 9.9% follow rate and 954 comments in 6 hours. This breaks down why, beat by beat, and turns it into a template for every episode.

## Episode 1, beat by beat
| # | Time | Beat | Line | Job it did |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–3.4 s | **Scale hook** | "This bunker has room for 100,000 people." + the "100,000 EMPTY BEDS" card | One huge, concrete number. The biggest like spike (0–2 s). |
| 2 | 3.5–5.6 s | **Contradiction tease** | "And every single one is empty." + a red "…almost." and a red pulse below | A curiosity gap: *almost?* Promises the twist by 6 s. |
| 3 | 6.6–8.6 s | **The rule (stakes)** | "The blast door won't seal until it's full." | A goal everyone shares |
| 4 | 9.0–11.1 s | **Question → "You."** | "So who sleeps down here? … You." | Direct address; the viewer becomes the hero (the gold YOU bed) |
| 5 | 11.6–14.3 s | **The deal** | "Everyone who follows gets a bed, with their name on the door." | The follow ask, told as story. Second like spike (13–14 s). |
| 6 | 15.2–24.4 s | **Milestone ladder** | 1,000 lights · 10,000 air · 100,000 the door seals | Progress, and a reason to bring others. *Retention steps down at 15 s.* |
| 7 | 24.4–26.1 s | (1.7 s silence) | | **Cost about 10% of viewers.** Don't repeat. |
| 8 | 26.1–27.8 s | **The turn** | "But there's something I haven't told you." | Resets attention for the second half |
| 9 | 28.3–32.0 s | **The secret** | "We thought this bunker had seven levels. Then we found an eighth." | New information that breaks what we were told |
| 10 | 32.7–37.4 s | **The forbidden thing** | "Only one bed down there. Bed 0. Locked from the inside." + knocks | The mystery object. From here, retention stays flat. |
| 11 | 38.9–40.6 s | **The guilty act** | "So we welded the hatch shut." | The narrator is complicit, which makes them interesting |
| 12 | 41.8–49.3 s | **Comment ask, 2 + 1** | "Pick your spot: by the garden, next to the armory, or on B7, right above bed 0." | Two attractive options and one dare, each with a swoop. Easy to comment. |
| 13 | 49.6–53.2 s | **Threat-wrapped follow** | "Follow me and get inside before bed 0 wakes up." | The follow ask again, with fear, then a push to the threat and a loop |

**Why it works in one sentence:** a giant promise (beds for everyone), a personal stake (yours, with your name), a shared goal (the milestones), a secret (B8), and an effortless action (pick a spot).

## The template (40–50 s)
Every episode must work for someone who has **never seen the show**: 95% of episode 1's viewers were non-followers. So every episode re-recruits as well as moving the story on.

| Beat | Time | What goes here | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Hook** | 0–3 s | The strangest true thing in this episode, in motion | Mystery visible in the first 2 s; one big number or one shocking line |
| **B. Tease** | 3–6 s | A red flash or a line that promises the twist | "…almost." energy; never resolve it here |
| **C. Recruit** | 6–12 s | The deal in one line for new viewers: "100,000 beds. Follow and one's yours." | **Every episode.** One line, no more. |
| **D. Progress** | 12–16 s | Where the counter is and the next milestone | **One** line, not a ladder (the ladder caused the 15 s drop) |
| **E. Showcase** | 16–21 s | Roll call: 4–6 real survivors, door plates and a comment card | Music, no voice; make the names readable |
| **F. Explore** | 21–31 s | Swoop into **one level**: what's there and who sleeps there | Show detailed props; the level's survivors get the roll call |
| **G. Turn + clue** | 31–38 s | "But…" → one new clue, found on that level | One clue only; never an answer; no silence before it |
| **H. Forbidden echo** | 38–41 s | Bed 0 or B8 reacts: a red glow, a knock, the heartbeat blip | Keeps the core mystery alive every episode |
| **I. Ask (2 + 1)** | 41–46 s | A comment ask with two attractive options and one dare | Rotate the type: spot, number, vote, theory, item |
| **J. Closer** | 46–50 s | A share or follow ask wrapped in the threat, then three knocks and a loop | Name a person to send it to: "the one you're bringing down" |

## The recruitment drive (episodes 2–7)
Each episode visits one level, showcases that level's survivors, recruits new ones, and finds one clue there.

| Ep | Level visited | Showcase | Clue found there | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 · One Too Many | The whole bunker (lights on) | First roll call, named beds | The counter counts **heartbeats**, and it's one over | Armory's full: garden, pantry or B7? |
| 3 · The Garden | **B3** | Garden survivors | The deepest roots grow **down**, through the floor, toward something warm | Name one thing you'd grow / pick your level |
| 4 · Command | **B2** (radio, med bay, archives) | The Overseer reveal + B2 survivors | The original blueprint labels B8 **"KEEPER"** | Theory: who was the Keeper? |
| 5 · The Armory | **B6** | B6 survivors and their dogs | The red crate holds **old survivor tags with bed numbers**. Someone slept here before. | Your one personal item |
| 6 · The Pantry | **B4** (commons, the stadium) | Soccer match / B4 survivors | Decades-old tins, eaten, and **tally marks** under the tables | Seal it or open it? (a running vote) |
| 7 · Engineering | **B7** (generators, above the hatch) | The bravest: B7 and the bed 100,000 holder | Through the floor, the three knocks are words: **DON'T. FILL. IT.** | "Do we keep going?" |
| (B1 and B5) | Entry and the dormitory | New arrivals, the dorm | Use for shorts between episodes | |

## Checklist for every script
- [ ] Hook in motion within 2 s; the twist promised by 6 s
- [ ] The deal restated in one line for new viewers
- [ ] One progress line, not a list
- [ ] Roll call of 4–6 real survivors
- [ ] One level explored, with its own survivors
- [ ] One clue, found on that level; no answers
- [ ] Bed 0 / B8 echo
- [ ] One ask (2 + 1), with the type different from the last episode
- [ ] A named share prompt
- [ ] No silence over about 0.5 s; 40–50 s total
- [ ] Ends on the threat, three knocks, loops
