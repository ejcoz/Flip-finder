# The Water Tanks — treatment

Halftone Dossier, vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, ~33.4 s. Narration: the user's own recording (`narration.mp3`, 32.7 s), used untouched, delayed 0.6 s so the payoff frame lands before the first word. The film ends 0.35 s after the last spoken word ("existed.") on the CLOSED stamp. No title card.

## Three candidate structures

1. **Chronological case file**: loan → man → certification → trick → collapse. Plain, follows the voice line by line.
2. **Payoff first, then the file** (chosen): open on the cutaway (the answer), then the file explains how anyone believed it, coming back to the same tank as the "evidence" in the middle, and ending on the arithmetic (1 of 16 tanks). The user asked for this hook, and repeating the tank turns it into a motif. The audience knows the answer up front and watches the inspectors miss it.
3. **The inspector's checklist**: told through one inspector's clipboard. Clever, but the narration is not written from his point of view.

## Logline and arc

A refinery borrows $150 million against tanks that hold water under a skin of oil.
Setup (0–14 s): money, man, certificate, bankers. Turn (14–26 s): the cutaway shows the truth; the inspector looks at the top; the pipes move the oil along. Ending (26–33 s): headline, sixteen tanks with one full, file stamped CLOSED.

## Benchmark

- *Learn from* 1960s newspaper photo-engraving and Ben-Day comic panels (halftone as the only shading); explainer shorts on financial fraud for vertical pacing (one image per sentence, captions as the spine).
- *Don't take*: any existing character, logo or layout. "American Express" appears only as plain set type on a stamp, never as a logo.

## Look

Manila paper #efe3c6; dark ink #1c2230 (outlines, text, caption pill); water = cobalt #2f6fa3 halftone; oil = amber #d38a1c with dark overprint; highlight yellow #f2c230 (keywords); vermilion #d23c2a (stamps, alarms); overprint shadow = cobalt. Five inks plus paper.
Headlines Alfa Slab One with a +8/+8 misregistered cobalt shadow; numerals Bagel Fat One; HUD JetBrains Mono 800; captions Archivo Black.

## Captions

Bold sans (Archivo Black), 74 px, in a dark-ink pill with a cobalt offset shadow, centred at y = 1248 (65 %). 2–4 words per card, timed to word starts from Whisper alignment, keyword in yellow. Numbers as digits: "$150 million", "1963", "1/16". Nothing below y = 1536 (bottom 20 %). The HUD sits at the top.
