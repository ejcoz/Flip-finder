# Baker Street, 1971 — Treatment

Halftone Dossier · vertical 1080×1920 · 30 fps · 35.1 s · the user's own narration, used as recorded (leading 0.06 s of silence trimmed; first word at ≈0.01 s).

## Three candidate structures

1. **The intercepted tape.** The film is a reel being played back in a police listening room: each exhibit is "what the tape said", ending on what it didn't say. Strong hook (the officer with headphones), but risks leaving the dig itself as an aside.
2. **The case file, read in order** (chosen). A manila dossier is opened exhibit by exhibit: street → cutaway → rooftop → listener → the gap in the evidence → the search → the verdict. Each beat of the narration is one exhibit card. It follows the user's 11 scenes exactly, keeps the twist for the last line, and the dossier framing makes the bureaucratic failure (750 banks checked) the turn.
3. **The countdown.** A running counter of boxes being emptied in the corner throughout. Rejected: it gives away the twist the brief asks us to hold back.

**Why 2:** the brief already fixes the order of exhibits; the dossier lets the "LOCATION: UNKNOWN" note and the 268 stamp be physical objects in the same file, so the ending is the file's verdict stamp, an echo of the hook text's promise.

## Logline and arc

The police could hear the Baker Street raid happening on tape, but not where it was. Setup (the dig, the lookout) → turn (the tapes never named the bank; 750 checked) → ending (the right vault, 268 boxes already empty).

## Benchmark

- *Ben-Day pop-art comics and 1970s British tabloid front pages*: learn dot density as light, slab headlines, a red that only means alarm. Don't take: any panel, character, masthead or logo.
- *Case-file title sequences of procedural TV*: learn exhibits sliding in one at a time, stamped verdicts. Don't take: any title design, type design or music.

## Look

Palette (five inks + paper): manila paper `#efe2c4` · sepia-black ink `#231a14` (outlines, caption pill) · teal `#1f6f78` (overprint shadow, radio waves) · mustard `#e0a526` (highlight, keyword ink, lamp light) · vermilion `#d8432c` (stamps, REC light, pins only). Night scenes (rooftop, listener's window, map) switch to a sepia-black ground with a teal/mustard dot layer, still multiplied by the paper. Real people are solid silhouettes, no faces.

## Cue map and score

96 BPM, D minor (Dm–Bb–Gm–A), synth pop-print toolkit at low level: triangle pad + soft square bass in 8ths, marimba-like plucks, finger snaps for the night section (15.5–23.7), a rising tick pattern under the pin search (26.4–29.4). Music sits ≥12 dB under the voice whenever the voice is active (ducked by a voice-activity envelope, measured per 100 ms window), and drops out entirely at 31.4 so the last line lands alone.

## Sound design table

Ambience beds were dropped: under the brief's "no effects over spoken words" a bed would sit under every word. The score carries the texture; effects live only in the pauses.

| Section | Effects (pauses only, gated 30 ms inside each pause) | Music |
|---|---|---|
| 1 listening | none (nothing before or under the first word) | triangle pad fades in with the first word |
| 2 card + street | paper slide as the dossier card lands (4.02 s) | pad, square bass 8ths, soft kick |
| 3–4 tunnel/vault | whoosh into the cutaway (10.18 s); thump + crumble as the wall gives (14.90 s) | bass + marimba plucks |
| 5–7 night | walkie click and chirp (18.74 s) | bass + finger snaps |
| 8 unknown | cassette eject clack (23.22 s), note slide (23.70 s), "?" stamp boom (26.02 s) | pad only |
| 9–10 search | four pin taps (29.03 s), whoosh into the zoom (29.36 s), vault door clunk (31.39 s) | ticks accelerating with the counter, then plucks |

Measured: music + effects are at least 14 dB under the voice in every 200 ms window of speech; effect energy over speech is zero.

## Type and captions

- Hook headline (shot 1, top): black slab serif (Alfa Slab One), ~92 px, per-character pop, teal misregistration shadow.
- Captions: bold sans (Archivo Black), ~74 px, 2–4 words per card, in a sepia-black pill, keyword in mustard, centred at y = 65 % (1248 px). Numbers as digits (1971, 40 ft, 750, 268). Nothing below y = 1536 (bottom 20 %).
- HUD: JetBrains Mono 800, case number "CASE 71/0911" top-left, REC dot + date top-right (hidden in shot 1 under the hook).
- Display numerals (counter, 268): Bagel Fat One.

No title card, no end card: the film ends on the last spoken word.
