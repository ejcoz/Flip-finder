# The Gardner Heist — treatment

Halftone Dossier · vertical 1080×1920 · 30 fps · 32.9 s (timed to the user's own narration, 32.7 s of speech starting at 0.00 s, ending on the last word).

## Three candidate structures

1. **Declassified report.** Each beat is a page unredacted one by one; the final page lifts its last bar to show the empty frame. Strong, but redaction bars fight the user's scene list (door, clock, basement), which is mostly staged action.
2. **Night timeline (chosen).** A case file that runs as a clock: 1:24 AM knock → 81-minute timer → the present day. Each exhibit is a staged print scene (night scenes on a navy ground, dossier cards on cream), stamped as it is proved. The clock gives the 12 user scenes a spine and lets the end jump from "that night" to "still today" in one cut.
3. **Evidence board pan.** One long pan down a pinned board, string linking exhibits. Elegant oner, but a single still board breaks the "something moves every 2 s" rule and buries the hook.

Chosen: **2**, because the user's scenes are already chronological and the jump from the timer/reward to the empty frame on the wall gives the twist a clean landing.

## Logline and arc

Two fake policemen talk their way into a Boston museum at night and walk out with 13 works that have never come back.
- Setup (0–11 s): the door, the date, the knock.
- Turn (11–18 s): the guard buzzes them in — they weren't police (FAKE stamp).
- Escalation (18–29 s): basement, 81 minutes, the cut canvases, 13 tiles, the unclaimed reward.
- Ending (30–32.9 s): the empty frame still on the wall, held back to the last line.

## Benchmark

- **Learn from** offset-printed 1960s true-crime magazine spreads and Saul Bass-era crime title cards: one silhouette, one flat light source, huge type, reduced colour. Also newspaper wire-photo halftone for night shading.
- **Don't take** any specific layout, logo, typeface design or shot. No reproduction of the stolen paintings: simplified halftone shapes only.

## Palette

Cream paper `#efe5cf` · navy ink `#1b2236` (outlines, night ground, caption pill) · cobalt `#2d5ea6` (overprint shadow) · gold `#e3ad37` (highlight, frames, light) · red `#d23c2c` (stamps, buzzer, alarm). Five inks plus paper.

## Cue map

84 BPM (0.714 s/beat). Pizzicato-like plucks and a muted square bass under a soft triangle pad; snaps for the sneaking section (18–25 s). Music sits ~18 dB under the voice (always ≥ 12 dB). Music starts with the voice at 0.00 (no lead-in), drops to near-silence at 29.2, and only a single low pad note sits under the last line.

## Subtitles and titles

- **Hook** (0–4.5 s): Alfa Slab One, ~118 px, three lines at the top, paper on navy with cobalt misregistration shadow, per-glyph pop already in place at frame 0 (letters settle from a small squash so it reads at once).
- **Captions**: Archivo Black ~78 px, 2–4 words, paper text in a navy pill with a cobalt offset shadow, keyword in gold; centred at 65 % height (y ≈ 1248). Numbers as digits (1990, 81, 13, $500 million). Nothing below y = 1536.
- **Dossier text**: JetBrains Mono 800; big numerals (timer, reward) Bagel Fat One.
- HUD top-left case chip "CASE 1990-03-18", top-right REC dot.
