# The Sun Is Shrinking — treatment

Style: Pixel Countdown Science. 1080×1920, 30 fps, 35.4 s, English, no voice.

## Why it hooks
The opening line contradicts what everyone assumes ("THE SUN IS SHRINKING") and asks the viewer to count along. It escalates through three mind-blowers in a row (mass becomes light → light is 8 minutes old → its energy is ~100,000 years old) and ends with a reason to remember it at the next sunrise.

## Three structures
1. **Count-along with escalating reveals** (chosen). 10 seconds of counting the Sun's mass loss, then two zoom-outs in time.
2. **Countdown challenge.** "Stare at a sunset for 8 minutes…" Rejected: it encourages looking at the Sun.
3. **Split verdict.** "Is the Sun burning like a fire?" (fusion vs combustion). Rejected: interesting, but no count to follow.

## Cast, setting, prop
- **Hero:** the Sun with a face, orange-red. It is deliberately *not* yellow, so yellow stays for numbers and key words.
- **Setting:** a starry night-indigo sky.
- **Prop:** a pile of Great Pyramids. One lands every ~1.4 s, because the Sun loses about one pyramid's mass that often.

## Beats
Hook (0–3.6) → 10-second count, +4.3 Mt per second, 7 pyramids land (3.6–13.6) → STOP and hard silence (13.6–14.1) → reveal "43 million tonnes" (14.1–18) → fuel for 5 billion years (18–21.8) → pull back: a photon flies Sun → Earth, 0:00 → 8:20 (21.8–26) → cut-away: a photon's random walk out of the core, 0 → 100,000 years (26–30.6) → CTA with a loop back to the opening frame (30.6–35.4).

## Facts and sources
- Mass converted to energy: L☉ / c² = 3.828×10²⁶ W / (3×10⁸ m/s)² ≈ 4.26×10⁹ kg/s ≈ **4.3 million tonnes per second** (IAU 2015 nominal solar luminosity). The Sun fuses ~600 million tonnes of hydrogen per second.
- Great Pyramid of Giza ≈ 5.9 million tonnes → one every ~1.39 s; 10 s ≈ 43 Mt ≈ 7 pyramids.
- Remaining main-sequence lifetime ≈ 5 billion years (NASA).
- Sunlight travel time ≈ 499 s ≈ 8 min 20 s (1 AU / c).
- Photon diffusion time from the core: estimates range from ~10,000 to ~170,000 years (NASA quotes ~100,000–170,000); the caption says "~100,000".
- "The Sun is shrinking" is literally true in mass. Its radius is not measurably shrinking from this loss; the 2-px shrink in the film is a visual joke, not a claim.
