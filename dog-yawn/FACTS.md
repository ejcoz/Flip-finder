## Not claimed
- That yawning proves dogs feel empathy (the authors say it *supports a link*).
- That any brain scan was done, or which brain areas are involved.
- That every dog catches yawns, or that dogs never yawn because they're tired or stressed.
- That the on-screen tallies are the study's real numbers.
