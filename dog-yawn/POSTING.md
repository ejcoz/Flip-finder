**Title**
Why Does Your Dog Yawn When You Yawn? 🥱🐶

**Description**
Most people think it's just tired or stressed. But in one study, 25 dogs watched their owner yawn, and a stranger yawn. They yawned more at their owner's yawns, and less when the yawn was fake.

And it probably wasn't stress: their heart rate stayed the same either way. In another study, 21 of 29 dogs caught a person's yawn. Why? Researchers can't pin down the mechanism, but your dog's brain may be tuned to you.

Your yawn is contagious, but mostly to the dog that knows you. Try it tonight: yawn slowly, and see if yours catches it. 🥱

Sources:
• Romero, Konno & Hasegawa, PLOS ONE (2013): 25 dogs, owner vs stranger yawns, heart rate
• Joly-Mascheroni, Senju & Shepherd, Biology Letters (2008): 21 of 29 dogs yawned after watching a person yawn
